# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework and no build step. Projects live in one data file (`js/projects.js`) so adding work later is a data edit. Chosen for load speed, any-host deployment, and full control over motion.

## Users

Founders, marketing leads, and owners of Saudi and regional companies who need a brand identity, a website, or a company profile. They arrive from a referral or a shared link, usually on a phone, and judge in the first screen whether the studio's own taste is at the level they want for themselves.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio site of WASB Studio (وثب). It introduces the studio, shows selected real work, and gets a visitor to start a project conversation. Success: the visitor opens at least one project and reaches the contact section.

## Positioning

"وثب" means a leap. The studio's claim is that it moves a client's identity and digital presence a clear step up, not that it supplies a single design. The site itself has to be the first proof of that level.

## Operating Context

Three services: logo and brand identity guidelines, website design, company profile design. The studio is led by Amr Ali, a creative director based in Riyadh.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Arabic first (RTL) with an English toggle.
- Contact details are not decided yet: placeholders live in one config block and must not be invented.
- Client work is shown as delivered: no redrawing, recolouring, or invented results, client names, sectors, or case-study claims.
- Mockups may reframe a work (device, spread, crop, background) but must keep the original design legible and faithful.
- Live websites are shown through real screenshots of the live sites.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: وثب / WASB Studio. Logo: a red arch frame with a rearing horse whose foreleg breaks out of the frame. The logo is used as supplied, never redrawn.
- The out-of-frame leap is the central idea the user wants carried through the site.
- Palette from the brief: brand red #C43B2B, charcoal #111111, warm white #F5F3EF. Red is an accent, not a fill for everything.
- Voice: studio "we" (نحن), short and direct.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the hero; the interface is its frame.
2. Nothing invented: every name, image, and claim traces to a supplied file.
3. The leap is shown, not described: things cross their frames.
4. Each work gets the presentation its nature calls for, never one repeated mockup.
5. Motion serves orientation and never delays reaching the content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced motion; the site stays fully understandable with effects off. Readable Arabic type sizes and solid contrast.
